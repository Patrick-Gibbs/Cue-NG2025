LTPIb estimates posterior mean genetic liability conditional on multiple binary PheCodes

LTPI generalises LTFH, which uses relatives' diagnoses instead of the person's other conditions. The paper simulates parent–offspring trios.

Not all comorbidity is genetic: why LTPI needs Σ

LDSC gives the genetic correlations (Ω) and its cross-trait intercepts give the environmental ones (Σ). Treating all shared risk as genetic biases the score.
